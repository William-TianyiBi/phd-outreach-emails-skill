# Email Pattern

## Student-First Email Shape

Subject: Prospective Fall 2027 PhD applicant interested in [topic]

Dear [Student Name],

I am Tianyi Bi, a junior undergraduate in Robotics Engineering at SUSTech, expecting to graduate in June 2027. I am exploring Fall 2027 PhD opportunities in robotic manipulation, especially [specific overlap].

I found your work on [student/lab project] closely aligned with my recent experience. My background includes [one project], [one result], and [one hands-on skill]. I am especially interested in how [lab-specific topic] can support scalable, robust manipulation.

I am writing to ask whether you would be open to briefly sharing your experience in Prof. [Name]'s group, and whether my background may be relevant to the group's prospective PhD recruiting direction. I have cc'ed Prof. [Name] for context.

Best regards,
Tianyi Bi

## Professor-Only Fallback

Use only when no relevant student can be identified.

Dear Prof. [Name],

I am Tianyi Bi, a junior undergraduate in Robotics Engineering at SUSTech, expecting to graduate in June 2027. I am exploring Fall 2027 PhD opportunities in robotic manipulation, especially [specific overlap].

[One paragraph linking lab work to Tianyi's strongest experience.]

I would be grateful to know whether your group may be considering PhD students in this direction for Fall 2027.

Best regards,
Tianyi Bi
